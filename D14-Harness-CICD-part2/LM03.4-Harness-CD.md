
---

# 37. IMPORTANT — PREVENT THE CI/CD LOOP

This is critical.

Your CI pipeline changes:

```text
manifests/python-deployment.yaml
```

and pushes it to GitHub.

Therefore the CD trigger should detect:

```text
manifests/**
```

But the CI trigger must NOT react to:

```text
manifests/**
```

Use:

```text
CI Trigger:
app/**
Dockerfile
requirements.txt
```

CD Trigger:

```text
manifests/**
```

Therefore:

```text
Application change
      ↓
CI
      ↓
Manifest change
      ↓
CD
```

and not:

```text
CI
 ↓
CI
 ↓
CI
 ↓
...
```
